Changelog

Changelog

Most requests from stores are decided and shipped the same day. Every update is free for every store.

  1. 1.52026-10-19

    Barcode check-out & check-in

    • Link blank QR labels or factory barcodes to gear — one code per unit, or one per model
    • Scan to count at check-out and check-in; wrong items are flagged on the spot
    • Return station: scan a pile of gear and it works out which orders it belongs to
    • Scan straight into a new order; phone cameras work as scanners
  2. 1.32026-10-18

    Customer-group pricing

    • Walk-in, partner and partner-rate groups, plus your own (students, annual clients…), each with a pricing rule and per-item overrides
    • One row per main item with accessories as compact tags — clear even with 100+ items
    • Crew-only orders, no gear required
    • Filter customers by group, outstanding balance and last rental
    • Open pages update themselves after a new release
  3. 1.22026-10-15

    Consumables and expenses

    • Consumables like tape: deducted on check-out, never returned, restocking logged as an expense
    • Add courier, delivery and fuel costs to any order; transport listed separately on the invoice
    • Release notes pop up once after each update
    • Each dashboard tile spells out exactly what needs doing
  4. 1.12026-10-14

    Statements, serial numbers, back-filling

    • Customer statements: print, save as image, or export to Excel
    • Bulk renumber and move serial numbers; pick the exact unit at check-in
    • Back-fill orders from before you started
    • Add forgotten accessories at check-in; damage recorded as minor, needs repair, or lost, with photos on the invoice
    • Sub-rent first, decide which partner later
    • Works in the built-in browsers of Huawei, Xiaomi and other Android phones
  5. 1.02026-10-13

    Launch

    • The full order flow: check-out, extensions, added gear, partial returns, payments and deposits
    • Billing by the clock; availability checked live so nothing is double-booked
    • Damage and repairs, crew clock-in, expenses and payroll, partner sub-rentals and reconciliation
    • Owner, warehouse and crew roles; desktop and phone, synced in real time
    • Daily backups, on the server and off-site
