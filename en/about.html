About

We shoot for a living, so we know what a gear rental house deals with every day.

Why we built RENTALWING

Beijing NEXWING Media is a film and interactive-media studio. Over six years we've made more than 160 commercials, brand films, documentaries and immersive pieces. We rent gear all the time, and we watched the rental houses we know run hundreds of items on spreadsheets and paper: what went out, when it's due back, whether the balance was paid — all kept in someone's head.

In 2026 we built this system for a gear rental house in Beijing, replacing a spreadsheet they had used for years. They logged over a hundred orders in the first week, and most of their requests shipped the same day. It worked so well that we turned it into RENTALWING for more rental houses.

What we believe

  • Software should follow how the store works, not the other way round
  • Hours saved and balances collected are what owners actually want
  • A store's data belongs to the store — export it any time, take it anywhere
